# Why Decentralized Prediction Markets?

## The Problem with Traditional Prediction Markets

Traditional prediction markets face several critical issues:

### 1. Complexity

- Require deep understanding of financial markets
- Complicated interfaces designed for traders
- High barrier to entry for casual users

### 2. Trust Issues

- Centralized platforms control outcomes
- Opaque resolution processes
- Users must trust the platform operator

### 3. Limited Social Features

- Focus on individual speculation
- No friend groups or communities
- Missing the social accountability aspect

### 4. High Costs

- Expensive gas fees for every action
- Minimum stakes too high for casual bets
- Platform fees eat into winnings

## The Decentralized Solution

### Transparency

All market outcomes are verifiable on-chain. No hidden manipulation.

### Low Costs

Movement Network provides:

- Fast transactions (~1 second finality)
- Low gas fees (fraction of a cent)
- Scalable infrastructure

### Community-Driven

- Groups assign their own judges
- Trust comes from social relationships
- Resolution based on real-world verification

### Accessibility

- Web2-like onboarding via Privy
- No need to understand blockchain
- Embedded wallets for seamless experience

## The Social Layer

Decentralization enables:

### Trustless Escrow

Stakes are locked in smart contracts, not controlled by any single party.

### Transparent Resolution

All outcomes recorded on-chain, visible to everyone.

### Community Governance

Groups can set their own rules and judges.

### Portable Reputation

Your prediction history follows you across platforms.

## Real-World Use Cases

### Personal Goals

"Will I finish my thesis by end of month?"

- Friends hold you accountable
- Small stakes make it meaningful
- Transparent outcome verification

### Group Challenges

"Who will lose the most weight this month?"

- Everyone stakes equally
- Judge verifies with photos/measurements
- Winner takes the pool

### Friendly Competitions

"Will our team win the tournament?"

- Group members bet on outcome
- Judge confirms official results
- Instant payout after resolution

## Beyond Simple Betting

### No-Loss Markets

- Stakes go into yield-generating strategies
- Only the yield is distributed as rewards
- Principal is always returned
- Perfect for risk-averse users

### Yield-Earning Markets

- Stakes earn interest while market is active
- Adds utility beyond simple wagering
- Incentivizes longer-term predictions
