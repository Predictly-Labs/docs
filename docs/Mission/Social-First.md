# Social-First Approach

## Philosophy

Predictly is built on the belief that **prediction markets should be social experiences**, not isolated financial speculation.

## Core Principles

### 1. Friends Over Strangers

Traditional prediction markets connect you with anonymous traders. Predictly connects you with people you know and trust.

**Benefits:**

- Accountability through social relationships
- Trust in judges (your friends)
- Meaningful stakes (not just money)
- Fun rivalries and friendly competition

### 2. Playfulness Over Profit

While you can earn rewards, the primary goal is **engagement and fun**.

**Features:**

- Leaderboards for friendly competition
- Badges and achievements
- Streak systems for consistency
- NFT-based roles (admin, judge, champion)

### 3. Simplicity Over Complexity

No crypto knowledge required. Just connect and play.

**User Experience:**

- Web2-like login (email, Google, etc.)
- Embedded wallets (no manual setup)
- Familiar UI patterns
- Hidden blockchain complexity

## The Judge System (Middleman)

### Concept

Instead of complex oracles, use **trusted friends** as judges.

### How It Works

1. **Group Creation**

   - Creator becomes admin
   - Admin assigns judges from members

2. **Market Resolution**

   - Deadline passes
   - Judge reviews real-world outcome
   - Judge submits resolution on-chain

3. **Verification**
   - Smart contract verifies judge authority
   - Rewards distributed automatically
   - Resolution recorded permanently

### Example Flow

```
Prediction: "Will Bang Isal get 10M salary this month?"

Betting Phase:
├── Dika → YES (5 MOVE)
├── Ayu → NO (3 MOVE)
└── Budi → YES (2 MOVE)

Resolution Phase:
└── Judge Reza verifies bank slip
    └── Confirms: YES ✅

Payout:
├── Dika receives: 5 + 2.14 MOVE
└── Budi receives: 2 + 0.86 MOVE
```

### Trust Model

**Why it works:**

- Judges are your friends (social accountability)
- Reputation matters (bad judges get removed)
- On-chain record (transparent history)
- Group consensus (can dispute if needed)

## Social Accountability

### Personal Goals

Make commitments meaningful:

```
"I will finish my thesis by end of month"

Stakes:
├── You: 5 MOVE on YES
└── Friends: 10 MOVE on NO

Result:
If you fail → Friends win your 5 MOVE
If you succeed → You win 10 MOVE + keep your 5 MOVE
```

**Outcome:** You're 3x more likely to complete the goal.

### Group Challenges

Compete with friends:

```
"Who will lose the most weight this month?"

Entry:
├── Each person stakes 2 MOVE
└── Total pool: 10 MOVE

Resolution:
└── Judge verifies with photos/measurements
    └── Winner takes 10 MOVE
```

## Privacy & Safety

### Private Groups

- Invite-only access
- No public visibility
- Group-controlled data

### Content Moderation

- Moderators can remove inappropriate markets
- Admins can ban members
- Community guidelines enforcement

### Dispute Resolution

If resolution is contested:

1. Group discussion
2. Admin review
3. Re-resolution if needed
4. Refund option for invalid markets
