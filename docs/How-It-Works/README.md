# How Predictly Works

Get started with Predictly in minutes. No crypto knowledge required.

## Quick Overview

1. **Connect Wallet** - Sign in with email or wallet
2. **Join/Create Group** - Find friends or create your own
3. **Browse Markets** - See what people are predicting
4. **Place Votes** - Stake MOVE on YES or NO
5. **Wait for Resolution** - Judge determines outcome
6. **Claim Rewards** - Winners get their share

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

## Key Concepts

### Markets

YES/NO predictions on any topic with a deadline.

### Stakes

MOVE tokens you bet on your prediction.

### Judges

Trusted friends who verify outcomes.

### Rewards

Winners split the losing pool proportionally.
