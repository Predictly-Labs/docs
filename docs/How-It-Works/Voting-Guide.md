# Voting Guide

Complete guide to placing votes and maximizing your prediction success.

## Overview

Voting is how you participate in prediction markets. Stake MOVE tokens on YES or NO, and win rewards if you're correct!

## Quick Start

1. Find a market
2. Choose YES or NO
3. Enter stake amount
4. Review potential reward
5. Confirm transaction
6. ✅ Vote placed!

---

## Understanding Markets

### Market Information

Before voting, check:

**Title & Description**

- What's the prediction?
- How will it be resolved?
- Is criteria clear?

**Deadline**

- When does voting close?
- Enough time left?

**Pool Size**

- Total MOVE staked
- Larger pool = more liquidity

**Odds**

- YES percentage
- NO percentage
- Current sentiment

**Participants**

- How many voters?
- Who's voting?

**Judge**

- Who will resolve?
- Are they trustworthy?

## How to Vote

### Step 1: Select Market

**Browse markets:**

- Public markets: "Explore" tab
- Group markets: "My Groups"
- Filter by category, time, status

**Click on market** to see details

### Step 2: Choose Your Prediction

**YES or NO?**

Consider:

- Your conviction level
- Available evidence
- Time until deadline
- Judge reliability

**Example:**

```
Market: "Will Bitcoin reach $100k by end of 2025?"

YES if you think:
- Bull market continuing
- Institutional adoption growing
- Historical patterns suggest it

NO if you think:
- Market correction likely
- Regulatory concerns
- Too ambitious target
```

### Step 3: Enter Stake Amount

**How much to stake?**

**Minimum:** Usually 0.1 MOVE  
**Maximum:** Set by market creator (or unlimited)

**Considerations:**

- Your conviction level
- Risk tolerance
- Available balance
- Potential reward

**Example stakes:**

```
Low conviction: 0.1-0.5 MOVE
Medium conviction: 0.5-2 MOVE
High conviction: 2-10 MOVE
Very high conviction: 10+ MOVE
```

### Step 4: Review Potential Reward

**Calculator shows:**

- Your stake
- Current odds
- Potential reward
- Profit if you win

**Example:**

```
Your vote: YES
Stake: 1 MOVE
Current pool: YES 50 MOVE, NO 30 MOVE
Your potential reward: 1 + (1/50 × 30) = 1.6 MOVE
Profit: 0.6 MOVE (+60%)
```

**Note:** Odds change as others vote!

### Step 5: Confirm Transaction

1. Click **"Place Vote"**
2. Review transaction details
3. Approve in wallet
4. Wait for confirmation (5-10 seconds)
5. ✅ Vote confirmed!

**Gas fee:** ~$0.01 (very cheap on Movement)

## Reward Calculation

### How Rewards Work

**Winners split the losing pool proportionally.**

**Formula:**

```
Your Reward = Your Stake + (Your Stake / Total Winning Stake) × Total Losing Stake
```

### Example 1: Simple Case

```
Market: "Will it rain tomorrow?"

Betting:
YES Pool: 10 MOVE (you: 5 MOVE)
NO Pool: 10 MOVE

Result: YES wins

Your reward:
= 5 + (5/10 × 10)
= 5 + 5
= 10 MOVE

Profit: 5 MOVE (+100%)
```

### Example 2: Uneven Pools

```
Market: "Will Bitcoin reach $100k?"

Betting:
YES Pool: 60 MOVE (you: 10 MOVE)
NO Pool: 40 MOVE

Result: YES wins

Your reward:
= 10 + (10/60 × 40)
= 10 + 6.67
= 16.67 MOVE

Profit: 6.67 MOVE (+66.7%)
```

### Example 3: Underdog Wins

```
Market: "Will underdog team win?"

Betting:
YES Pool: 20 MOVE (you: 5 MOVE)
NO Pool: 80 MOVE

Result: YES wins (underdog!)

Your reward:
= 5 + (5/20 × 80)
= 5 + 20
= 25 MOVE

Profit: 20 MOVE (+400%!)
```

**Lesson:** Betting on underdogs = higher potential rewards!

## Voting Strategies

### Strategy 1: Follow Your Conviction

**Best for:** Personal predictions, things you know well

**Approach:**

- Research thoroughly
- Trust your analysis
- Stake according to confidence
- Don't follow the crowd

**Example:**

```
You're a weather expert in Jakarta.
Market: "Will it rain in Jakarta tomorrow?"
You check forecast: 90% chance of rain
→ Vote YES with high stake
```

### Strategy 2: Contrarian Betting

**Best for:** Markets with herd mentality

**Approach:**

- Look for overvalued sides
- Bet against the crowd
- Higher risk, higher reward
- Need strong conviction

**Example:**

```
Market: "Will Bitcoin crash?"
YES: 80 MOVE (80%)
NO: 20 MOVE (20%)

If you think crash unlikely:
→ Bet NO for 4x potential return
```

### Strategy 3: Diversification

**Best for:** Risk management

**Approach:**

- Spread stakes across multiple markets
- Different topics, timeframes
- Reduces overall risk
- Steady returns

**Example:**

```
Total budget: 10 MOVE

Split across:
- Sports: 2 MOVE
- Crypto: 2 MOVE
- Personal goals: 2 MOVE
- Weather: 2 MOVE
- Politics: 2 MOVE
```

### Strategy 4: Early Bird

**Best for:** Getting better odds

**Approach:**

- Vote early when market opens
- Odds more favorable
- Influence market sentiment
- Risk: Less information

**Example:**

```
Market just created:
YES: 1 MOVE (50%)
NO: 1 MOVE (50%)

You vote YES with 5 MOVE:
YES: 6 MOVE (60%)
NO: 1 MOVE (40%)

Later voters see your confidence!
```

### Strategy 5: Wait and See

**Best for:** Uncertain markets

**Approach:**

- Watch market develop
- See what others think
- Vote closer to deadline
- Risk: Worse odds

**Example:**

```
Market: "Will new product launch succeed?"

Day 1: 50/50 split
Day 7: Product reviews out, 70% YES
Day 14: You vote YES with conviction
```

## Advanced Tips

### Reading Market Sentiment

**High YES percentage (70%+):**

- Crowd thinks YES likely
- Lower potential reward for YES
- Higher risk/reward for NO

**Balanced (45-55%):**

- Market uncertain
- Fair odds both sides
- Good for confident predictions

**High NO percentage (70%+):**

- Crowd thinks NO likely
- Lower potential reward for NO
- Higher risk/reward for YES

### Timing Your Vote

**Early (first 25% of time):**

- ✅ Better odds
- ✅ Influence sentiment
- ❌ Less information

**Middle (25-75%):**

- ✅ Balanced info/odds
- ✅ See market trend
- ❌ Average odds

**Late (last 25%):**

- ✅ Maximum information
- ✅ Clear trend
- ❌ Worse odds

### Stake Sizing

**Kelly Criterion (advanced):**

```
Optimal Stake % = (Probability × Odds - 1) / (Odds - 1)

Example:
You think YES has 70% chance
Current odds: 60% YES, 40% NO
Implied odds: 1.67x

Optimal stake = (0.7 × 1.67 - 1) / (1.67 - 1)
= 0.169 / 0.67
= 25% of bankroll
```

**Simpler approach:**

- Low conviction: 1-5% of bankroll
- Medium: 5-10%
- High: 10-20%
- Very high: 20-30%
- Never: >50%

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Chasing Losses

**Problem:** Betting more to recover losses

**Example:**

```
Lost 5 MOVE → Bet 10 MOVE to recover
Lost 10 MOVE → Bet 20 MOVE to recover
→ Spiral of losses
```

**Solution:** Stick to your strategy, accept losses

### Mistake 2: FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)

**Problem:** Voting without research

**Example:**

```
Everyone voting YES → You vote YES too
But you didn't read criteria
Market resolves NO → You lose
```

**Solution:** Always read and understand

### Mistake 3: Ignoring the Judge

**Problem:** Not checking judge reliability

**Example:**

```
Market has unknown judge
Judge resolves unfairly
You lose despite being right
```

**Solution:** Only vote if you trust the judge

### Mistake 4: Betting Too Much

**Problem:** Staking more than you can afford

**Example:**

```
You have 10 MOVE total
You stake 9 MOVE on one market
Market resolves against you
→ Almost broke
```

**Solution:** Never stake >20% on single market

### Mistake 5: Not Reading Criteria

**Problem:** Misunderstanding resolution rules

**Example:**

```
Market: "Will Bitcoin reach $100k?"
You think: Anytime ever
Criteria: By end of 2025 only
→ You misunderstood
```

**Solution:** Always read description carefully

## Tracking Your Votes

### My Predictions Dashboard

**View all your votes:**

1. Go to "My Predictions"
2. See active, pending, resolved

**For each vote:**

- Market title
- Your prediction (YES/NO)
- Stake amount
- Current status
- Potential reward

### Vote Status

**🟢 ACTIVE**

- Voting still open
- Can't change your vote
- Watch market develop

**🟡 PENDING**

- Deadline passed
- Waiting for judge
- Resolution coming soon

**✅ WON**

- You predicted correctly!
- Claim your reward
- See profit amount

**❌ LOST**

- You predicted incorrectly
- Stake goes to winners
- Learn for next time

**⚪ INVALID**

- Market cancelled
- Everyone refunded
- No winners/losers
